# Dekho

A private, ad-free home screen for TCL Google TVs. One screen, no scrolling:
streaming apps, HDMI inputs with live status, one-press brightness, Wi-Fi and headphone status.
Tested on one TCL 55C61B (Android TV 12) only.

- Press Home on the remote to come back to Dekho from any app.
- Press Bluetooth to connect your paired headphones. Hold it to open the Bluetooth page.
- Press Google TV to visit Google's screen. Hold it to make Google TV the home screen again.
  Open Dekho from Apps to switch back.

Build: `gradle assembleRelease` with `ANDROID_HOME` set and `ANDROID_SDK_ROOT` unset.
